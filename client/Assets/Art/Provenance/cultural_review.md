## Method notes

- Inscriptions are text styles rendered on the Guild Stone entity (cosmetics.md);
  review covers the calligraphy motif naming, not glyph artwork.
- Paid cosmetics (IAP + season paid) depict archetypes only; none name a
  historical or deified person (cosmetics.md invariant).
- Emote `cosmetic.iap.emote.ngoi_thien_dinh` (meditation sit) is a generic seated
  pose; it does not depict a specific living religion's sacred rite or vestment.
- Glow/aura/trail assets are abstract light/color effects; motif names are
  Vietnamese folk colour terms, no sacred geometry or symbol is reproduced.
- Any cosmetic receiving a NO-GO would have been replaced with an alternative
  motif or re-scoped to text-only; none required it.
