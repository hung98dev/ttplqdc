# ADR-0065: Data Schema Completion, Erasure and Retention
status: ACCEPTED

## Context
The round-2 network/data/security review found that the erasure transaction could not commit (composite FKs of `account_entitlement_claims` block re-pointing `account_id`; a second erased owner of the same season track collides on the partial unique index; 8-hex placeholder names collide under `UNIQUE(name_key)` and the prefix was claimable by players; Category A deletion was blocked by RESTRICT FKs); retention periods contradicted each other between `data_protection.md` and `personal_data_register.md`; the character name limit was 32 graphemes in one file and 16 in another with a `name_key` column too short for case-fold expansion; `operations`, auth, IAP dedup, audit, reward-claim, auction and guild tables had no column/key schema, and a global `UNIQUE(operation_id)` collided with per-character simulation settlements; relic expiry and relic creation locked marker and relic rows in opposite orders; the account-takeover and `ECONOMY_REVIEW` signals had no persisted data. The architecture review also found that an empty WorldConsequence aggregate was both "valid" and a "hard failure", and the regression review found that earned PUBLIC-boss chest rewards were stranded by a restart.

## Decision
- **Erasure:** one transaction (`data_model.md` § Account Erasure) with `SET CONSTRAINTS ALL DEFERRED`; personal rows deleted; financial/relational rows re-pointed to the baseline-seeded `TOMBSTONE_ACCOUNT_ID`, which is excluded from the season-track unique index; the two composite FKs of `account_entitlement_claims` are `DEFERRABLE INITIALLY IMMEDIATE` (no `ON UPDATE` cascades anywhere); names become `Anonymized_` + 32-hex character UUID and the `anonymized_` key prefix is reserved; guild detach with deterministic leadership transfer or sole-member disband; the residual `accounts` row (no personal column) is purged 1 year after `erased_at`.
- **Retention:** `personal_data_register.md` § 1 is the single retention schedule; `data_protection.md` references it. Rate-limit counters are hash-keyed, expire within 24 h and are not subject-searchable. Settlement/event records roll off after 180 days; value-holding rows never.
- **Names:** character 1..16 graphemes and ≤ 64 UTF-8 bytes; guild 1..24 graphemes and ≤ 96 bytes; `name_key VARCHAR(256)` (`text.md` § Name Limits).
- **Schemas:** `operations` PK `(operation_family, owner_id, operation_id)` (client generates `operation_id` for client-initiated requests, `ids.md`); `account_identities`, `auth_session_families`, `auth_refresh_credentials`, `auth_revocations` (access credentials, gameplay tickets and resume credentials are process memory only); `account_login_history`; `accounts.erased_at`, `credential_guard_until`, `economy_review_flagged_at`; `iap_notification_dedup`, `iap_provider_cursors`; typed `account_iap_entitlements` (`PENDING -> REFUNDED` allowed; Steam `PENDING` re-queried by `QueryTxn` every 15 min; never-approved `Init` orders become `REJECTED` 24 h after `InitTxn`) and `account_entitlement_claims` with a PK; `audit_events`; `reward_claims` + `reward_claim_lines` + `reward_claim_contributions`; `auction_listings`; guild tables; `trade_settlement_records.trade_id` without FK.
- **Locking:** within a priority, tables lock in listed order; relic/marker transactions lock `region_di_tich_markers` first.
- **WorldConsequence load:** zero rows is valid; expired relics and stale markers are repaired at load; unreadable tables or unknown content IDs fail closed; `WORLD_CONSEQUENCE_LOAD_TIMEOUT = 5 s`.
- **PUBLIC chest eligibility:** rows carry their copy; defeated-copy rows settle into Reward Claims at chest despawn or process start; undefeated-copy rows are deleted.
- **Backups:** erasure-ledger hash uses `ERASURE_LEDGER_SALT`; ledger stored in the pgBackRest repository (`BACKUP_STORAGE_URL`, `BACKUP_STORAGE_CREDENTIALS_FILE`, ADR-0066).

## Amendment (ADR-0070)
Erasure ledger entries are written as `pending_erasure_ledger` rows inside the erasure transaction and PUT by a sweeper as `erasure-ledger/<operation_id>.json`; erasure takes its lock set in priority order and has a `LEDGER_REPLAY` mode; the refund score suspends only `ACTIVE` accounts (`../06_data/data_model.md` § Account Erasure).

## Amendment (schema completion: social and competitive tables)
Typed schemas for `friends`, `friend_requests`, `blocks`, `character_chivalry`, `pvp_ratings`, `pvp_match_settlements`, `pvp_sanctions`, `guild_war_ratings` and `guild_war_settlements` (`../06_data/data_model.md` § Social / Party, § PvP / Guild War); caps of 100 outgoing pending friend requests and 500 blocks (`../03_systems/social.md`, `CAPACITY_FULL` on messages 611/617/654); retention in `../07_security/personal_data_register.md` Category G; `character_chivalry` locks at priority 2 (`../06_data/database.md`). Packets: IMP-034, IMP-040, IMP-041, IMP-042, IMP-086.
